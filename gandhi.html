Mahatma Gandhi

A lawyer who developed satyagraha, a method of resisting injustice through nonviolent action, and used it to challenge colonial rule.

Indian block-printSaffron and green with a repeating teardrop, the buti shape found in block-printed Indian textiles.

Lawyer and leader of nonviolent resistance

  • Began developing satyagraha during his years in South Africa before returning to India in 1915.
  • Led the Salt March in 1930, walking about 240 miles in 24 days from Sabarmati to Dandi to protest the salt tax.
  • India became independent on 15 August 1947, after decades of campaigns he helped lead.

Key dates

  1. 1893Arrives in South Africa to work as a lawyer.
  2. 1915Returns to India.
  3. 1930Leads the Salt March, 12 March to 6 April.
  4. 1942Calls for the Quit India movement.
  5. 1947India becomes independent on 15 August.
  6. 1948Assassinated in Delhi on 30 January.

Why they endure. His birthday, 2 October, is marked by the United Nations as the International Day of Non-Violence.
