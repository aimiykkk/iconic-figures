Tunku Abdul Rahman

A prince of Kedah who negotiated Malaya's path to independence and became its first Prime Minister.

Statesman and first Prime Minister of Malaysia

  • Led the independence negotiations with Britain and proclaimed Merdeka at Stadium Merdeka, Kuala Lumpur, on 31 August 1957.
  • Led the formation of Malaysia on 16 September 1963, joining Malaya with Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore.
  • Remained Prime Minister until 1970 and is remembered as Bapa Kemerdekaan, the Father of Independence.

Key dates

  1. 1903Born in Alor Setar, Kedah.
  2. 1951Becomes president of UMNO.
  3. 1955Leads the Alliance to victory in the first federal election.
  4. 1957Proclaims independence on 31 August.
  5. 1963Malaysia is formed on 16 September.
  6. 1970Steps down as Prime Minister.
  7. 1990Dies in Kuala Lumpur.

Why they endure. Every 31 August and 16 September in Malaysia traces back to decisions made under his leadership.
