Marie Curie

She moved from Poland to Paris to study, then built a research career on a phenomenon she named radioactivity.

Polish wycinankiMarie was born in Warsaw, so this page uses Polish folk paper-cut: red and green flowers, traditionally cut from paper to decorate cottage walls.

Physicist and chemist

  • With Pierre Curie, discovered the elements polonium and radium in 1898.
  • Won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903, shared with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel.
  • Won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1911, making her the first person to win in two scientific fields.
  • During the First World War she helped equip vehicles with X-ray units to treat wounded soldiers.

Key dates

  1. 1891Moves from Warsaw to Paris to study at the Sorbonne.
  2. 1895Marries Pierre Curie.
  3. 1898Announces polonium and radium.
  4. 1903Shares the Nobel Prize in Physics.
  5. 1911Wins the Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
  6. 1914–18Helps set up mobile X-ray units for field hospitals.
  7. 1934Dies in Passy, France.

Why they endure. She was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and remains the benchmark for scientific persistence.
