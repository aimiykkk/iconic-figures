A short glossary

Words from science, history and traditional art that appear on this website.

Analytical EngineEnglish · Ada Lovelace

Charles Babbage's design for a general-purpose mechanical computer. It was never completed.

ApartheidAfrikaans · Nelson Mandela

The system of legally enforced racial segregation in South Africa from 1948 until the early 1990s.

ButiIndian textiles · Gandhi's style

A small repeated flower or teardrop motif used in block-printed cloth.

MerdekaMalay · Tunku Abdul Rahman

The Malay word for independence.

NdebeleSouthern Africa · Mandela's style

A people whose women paint bold geometric patterns on the walls of their houses.

PolymathGreek origin · Leonardo da Vinci

A person with deep knowledge across many fields.

Pucuk rebungMalay · Tunku's style

Bamboo shoot. The triangle motif woven into songket cloth.

RadioactivityCoined by Marie Curie · Marie Curie

The release of energy from unstable atoms, such as radium.

SatyagrahaSanskrit · Mahatma Gandhi

Roughly 'holding firmly to truth'. Gandhi's method of nonviolent resistance.

SongketMalay · Tunku's style

Hand-woven Malay cloth brocaded with gold or silver thread.

WycinankiPolish · Curie's style

Polish folk paper cut-outs, traditionally used to decorate homes.
