Ada Lovelace

The daughter of the poet Lord Byron, she saw that a calculating machine could do more than arithmetic.

Born
10 December 1815, London
Died
27 November 1852, London
Age at death
36 years
Country
England
Victorian EnglandBurgundy and gold with a diamond lattice, the look of nineteenth-century drawing-room wallpaper and bookbinding.

Mathematician and writer

  • Worked with Charles Babbage and wrote extensive notes on his proposed Analytical Engine, published in 1843.
  • Her notes include a step-by-step method for calculating Bernoulli numbers, often called the first computer program.
  • Suggested that such a machine might one day work with symbols such as music, not only with numbers.

Key dates

  1. 1815Born in London, the only legitimate child of Lord Byron.
  2. 1833Meets Charles Babbage and sees his calculating machine.
  3. 1835Marries William King, later the Earl of Lovelace.
  4. 1843Publishes her notes on the Analytical Engine.
  5. 1852Dies in London at 36.

Why they endure. She imagined general-purpose computing a century before electronic computers existed.
