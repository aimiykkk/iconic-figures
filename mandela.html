Nelson Mandela

A lawyer and activist who spent 27 years in prison for opposing apartheid, then led South Africa into democracy.

Anti-apartheid leader and President

  • Was sentenced to life imprisonment in 1964 and released on 11 February 1990 after 27 years.
  • Shared the 1993 Nobel Peace Prize with F. W. de Klerk for ending apartheid peacefully.
  • Became South Africa's first president elected in fully democratic elections in 1994 and served until 1999.

Key dates

  1. 1944Helps form the ANC Youth League.
  2. 1962–64Arrested, then sentenced to life imprisonment after the Rivonia Trial.
  3. 1990Released on 11 February after 27 years.
  4. 1993Shares the Nobel Peace Prize with F. W. de Klerk.
  5. 1994Elected president in South Africa's first fully democratic election.
  6. 1999Leaves office after one term.
  7. 2013Dies in Johannesburg on 5 December.
